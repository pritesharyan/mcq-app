Privacy Policy

Last updated September 27, 2026

Information you provide

When you register or update your profile, the service collects your name, username, mobile number, email address, and password. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, not as the original text.

Practice and account activity

The service stores answers to practice questions, completed test results, suggestions you submit, and login-session activity. Administrators can see information needed to manage accounts; master administrators can also view session engagement.

How information is used

Information is used to provide quizzes and account features, save your progress, respond to suggestions, manage access, and send account-recovery details to your registered email address.

Cookies and service providers

A session cookie keeps you signed in. Email delivery may be handled by the service’s configured mail provider. Some pages load web fonts from a third-party provider, which may receive standard connection information such as your IP address.

Retention and your choices

Account and activity records are kept to operate the service. The app does not currently provide self-service account deletion. To ask about access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, contact us at goodintentltd@gmail.com.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The date above indicates when this page was last revised. Contact us with privacy questions or requests.
